# IRL Resources for Campaigning

A companion to the [FOSS tools list](resources.md). Software wins you sovereignty over your infrastructure; *this* page covers the work that happens off the screen — door-knocks, banner drops, jail support, zine tables, skill-shares, and the long quiet labour of building a base.

Curated for organizers in **Treaty 6, 7, and 8 territories** (Edmonton, Alberta, and across Canada) with a strong bias toward things that are **free** and toward the public-domain / anti-copyright corner of the movement library. Maintained by [The Bunker Operations](https://bnkops.com). Suggestions welcome — corrections especially welcome.

!!! tip "If you only follow five links"

    [**The Commons Social Change Library**](https://commonslibrary.org) (the meta-index — start here), [**Beautiful Trouble Toolbox**](https://beautifultrouble.org/toolbox), [**Sprout Distro PDF catalogue**](https://www.sproutdistro.com/catalog/pdfs/), [**CCLA Know-Your-Rights for Protesters**](https://ccla.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Know-Your-Rights-Protesters.pdf), and the [**Parkland Institute**](https://www.parklandinstitute.ca/) for the Alberta lens. Everything else on this page deepens one of those five.

The campaigning resources nearest to home. Most international FOSS and movement-library lists skip this part; this is where it goes.

!!! warning "Edmonton bylaws prohibit unauthorized postering on public property"

    Wheatpasting on city assets carries fines under [Bylaw 14600](https://www.edmonton.ca/city_government/bylaws). Community boards, university kiosks, and private-with-permission walls are the legal options. The legal-versus-illegal trade-off is yours to make — but make it knowingly.
